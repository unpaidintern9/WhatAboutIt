# Brand Notes

Visual direction:

- Vintage Americana.
- Nashville recording studio.
- Deep red, warm cream, black leather, aged paper, brass accents.
- Bold block typography and handwritten accent typography.
- Cute, spunky, confident, no-filter personality.

Image rule:

- Do not use fake people photos.
- Do not generate Morgan or guest photos.
- Use branded placeholders, textures, icons, type, and empty camera preview boxes.
