# BLUF

We are building one simple offline desktop app called What About It? Studio. Morgan opens it, clicks New Episode, records up to 3 cameras and multiple mics, presses Auto Edit, reviews the suggested edits, and exports the finished podcast. OBS, FFmpeg, MLT, whisper.cpp, and other tools stay hidden behind the scenes.

The app must feel like What About It? from the first screenshot: vintage Americana, Nashville recording studio warmth, deep reds, warm creams, black leather, aged paper, brass accents, bold block typography, handwritten accents, and confident conversational wording.

The app must not use fake people photos or generated Morgan/guest images. All visual scaffolding uses brand marks, textures, iconography, type, and empty camera preview boxes.
