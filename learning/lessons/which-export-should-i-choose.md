# Which export should I choose?

- **Full Episode Video** is ready for YouTube.
- **Audio Only** is an audio file for podcast platforms.
- **Archive Master** is the keep-forever local copy.
- **Social Clip** creates a vertical video from the range selected in Review.
- **Editor Handoff** packages the reference edit, full-length Program and cameras, isolated microphones, captions, sync notes, and transfer checksums for an outside editor.

For Editor Handoff, choose **Compatible MP4** for smaller transfers and broad compatibility, including CapCut. Choose **ProRes 422 MOV** for smoother editing in Premiere, Resolve, or Final Cut when you have room for much larger files.

The handoff preserves source video resolution, frame rate, and microphone channels. It supplies media and notes; your editor rebuilds the timeline against the reference video. Choose a destination, then press **Build editor handoff**.
