# How recovery works

For a normal break, press **Pause** and wait until the app says the episode is saved. You can then close the app.

Open the app later, choose the paused episode from Recent Episodes or **Open Paused Episode** in Record, and press **Resume**. Recording continues in the same episode folder. Time spent paused is left out.

Press **Stop** when the episode is finished. The app combines and verifies the recorded parts before Review or Export. This can take time for a long episode; keep the app open while Saving is shown.

If capture or saving was interrupted, use **Recover Recording**. Original parts remain protected for retry. Older recordings support recovery but cannot be reopened as a new resumable pause.
