# Why Files Are Saved Locally

Local saving means recording does not depend on the internet.

Your raw files stay on this computer.

The app does not upload recordings or send telemetry.


Each time you press Record after a previous session, the app creates a new recording folder automatically. An empty episode is used for its first recording. Pause saves the current part. Once saving completes you may close the app; reopening and Resume continue the same episode folder. Stop finishes the episode for Review and Export. The saving location appears in Live Studio, and Recent Episodes shows the date and time.
