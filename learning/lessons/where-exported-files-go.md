# Where exported files go

Every finished copy gets its own dated folder inside `Episode/Exports/`. That folder holds the video or audio, optional masters, captions, and export notes together. Export again to make another folder.

**Editor Handoff** creates a separate dated package in the destination you choose, such as an external drive or transfer folder.

The Export screen shows the exact location. Press **Open export folder** after completion to find that export. Earlier exports and original recordings stay safe.
